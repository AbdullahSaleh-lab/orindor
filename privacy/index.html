Privacy Policy — Orindor: Wizard VS Horde

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Orindor: Wizard VS Horde (“Orindor”) is a game for iPhone. It has no account of its own, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code. This policy explains what it keeps and what it sends.

What the game stores on your device

Your campaign progress and stars (Normal and Hard), your account level, seals, relics, quests and records, your Conquest in progress, the profile name you choose, and your settings (sound, music, effects, vibration, screen shake, flashes). They are kept in the app’s own storage on your device. Deleting the app removes them from the device.

Profiles in your iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your profile (its name, your progress, the type of device it is playing on, and a random ID for that device, used so a profile is open on one device at a time) is saved in your own private iCloud storage, so you can continue on another device signed in with the same Apple ID. This data is stored by Apple in your iCloud account under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we cannot see or access it. You can delete a profile in the game (Settings → Profiles), or manage the game’s iCloud data in your device’s Settings.

Gameplay statistics

Orindor collects anonymous gameplay statistics: which modes and stages are played, results, how long battles take, which spells and items are used, and how warm the device gets, together with the app version, the iOS version and the iPhone model family. Once a day they are sent to our server, hosted by Cloudflare. We do not store your IP address (Cloudflare’s network may process it briefly to deliver and protect the service). The statistics carry no name, account or contact details, and are linked only to a random number created when the game is installed (deleting the game discards it). We use them only to improve the game, on the basis of our legitimate interest in doing so, and delete them after 12 months.

Reporting a bug

The bug button on the home screen opens an email to us in your mail app, with the app version and device model filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself; we use what you write only to answer you and fix the problem.

Permissions

Orindor does not ask for your location, camera, photos, contacts, microphone, tracking or notifications.

Apple-provided data

The App Store gives developers aggregated, anonymous statistics such as download counts and crash reports, depending on your device’s analytics-sharing setting. These never identify you.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone; the gameplay statistics above contain none.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
