ORINDORWIZARD VS HORDE

One wizard. One gate.

A horde pours out of the Dark Marches and fills the field. Orindor, the realm's last wizard, stands on the wall between it and the town. Burn them, blast them off their feet, call down the storm, and keep the gate standing until the last wave breaks.

Your power

FIVE SPELLS

Fireball, Blast, Storm and Thunderbolt recharge as you fight. The Word, your greatest spell, charges as foes fall and devastates the whole field.

BLINK

Teleport out among the horde to reach the ones too far away, then Blink back to the wall.

TOWERS

Build crossbows, catapults and wind totems on the stones beside the road, and upgrade them as the gold comes in.

Ways to play

CAMPAIGN

Twelve stages across the river fords, the lava fields, the Broken Cliffs and the edge of the Marches, each with its own part of the story. Win it, and Hard mode opens: the same stages, with a bigger and tougher horde.

THE DAILY

A new twist every day that bends one rule of battle, the same for everyone. Each gives a gift and takes a cost.

STORM

Hold out for three minutes against a horde that never stops growing. Raise your Wrath, sound the Rally horn, and chase a grade from D to S.

CONQUEST

Win back the realm land by land, with archers on the wall instead of towers, then storm the Warlord's camp.
